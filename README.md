# 🎀 A Birthday Surprise for Maa
> A personalized, interactive birthday website created especially for my mother.

### [🎁 Open the Birthday Surprise](https://wonderful-druid-b97148.netlify.app)
---

## 💝 About the Project

This project is a personalized, interactive birthday experience created especially for my mother.

Rather than creating a conventional birthday greeting, I wanted to build a small web experience that reflects different aspects of her life, academic interests, professional journey, and the influence she has had on me.

Her birthday also falls on **15 August — India's Independence Day**, which inspired me to incorporate the theme of independence, determination, learning, and strength into the experience.

---

## ✨ Concept

The website follows a narrative structure rather than presenting everything on a single page.

Each section represents a different aspect of her journey, including:

- Her identity as a woman
- Astrology
- Psychology
- Philosophy
- Her academic journey
- Her PhD aspirations
- Independence Day
- A final personal message

The goal was to transform a personal story into an interactive digital experience.

## 🎨 Design Approach

The visual design was intentionally created around a soft, celebratory aesthetic.

Key design elements include:

- Pink and red accents
- White and gold backgrounds
- Decorative bows
- Birthday-themed visual elements
- Story-driven navigation
- Readable typography
- High-contrast text
- Mobile-friendly layouts
- Interactive buttons

---

## 📱 Responsive Design

The website was designed and tested for:

- Desktop
- Laptop
- Android/mobile devices

The mobile experience was specifically checked to ensure that the pages, typography, buttons, navigation, and interactive elements remain usable on smaller screens.
