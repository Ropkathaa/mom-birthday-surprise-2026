Born Beneath
A Free Sky.

Every year, the country celebrates Independence Day.
And every year, I get another reason to celebrate it.

✦

Not because she was born in 1947.
But because she was born on a day that reminds us what freedom means.

✦

To Choose

To make decisions for yourself.

♡

To Learn

To keep asking questions throughout life.

∞

To Become

To keep becoming whoever you choose to be.

And perhaps that is what makes her birthday so beautiful.

She was born on a day celebrating a nation's freedom —
and became a woman who never stopped thinking for herself.
