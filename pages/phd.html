SOME STORIES ARE NOT FINISHED

The Chapter
She Never Finished.

After her Master's in Philosophy, there was another dream waiting — the Doctor of Philosophy.

KNOWLEDGE
LIFE

And then life asked her to open another book.

— the book of motherhood.

She was young. I was young.

And the responsibilities of life changed the direction of a dream that had once been very close.

✦

But an unfinished degree does not mean an unfinished mind.

Because she never stopped studying.
She never stopped asking questions.
And she never stopped becoming a source of knowledge for others.
