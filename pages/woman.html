CHAPTER I

Before She Was
My Mother

She was a woman who chose to keep learning.

“

A woman of her time, bold enough to make her own choices, determined enough to keep walking when the path was not obvious.

”

She studied. She questioned. She explored. She chose philosophy, became an astrologer, developed a deep interest in psychology, and continued learning long after formal education had ended.

And somehow, the woman who spent her life searching for knowledge became the person who gave me mine.
