FINAL CHAPTER

FROM YOUR DAUGHTER

My First
Teacher.

✦

Dear Maa,

I wanted to give you something today that was a little different.

Not just a birthday wish. Not just a bouquet of words. But something that could remind you of the woman I see when I look at you.

I see a woman who chose her own path. A woman who kept studying, kept questioning, and kept learning even when life did not always make the path easy.

I see the astrologer who helps people find a direction, the philosopher who never stopped asking questions, and the person who has always believed that knowledge is worth pursuing.

And perhaps most importantly, I see the person who became my first source of knowledge.

Long before I understood what education meant, you were already teaching me. Long before I knew how to find direction, you were already showing me one.

You have been a source of guidance not only for me, but for so many people who have trusted you with their questions, their worries, and their hopes.

And even today, you keep learning. That is one of the things I admire most about you.

Perhaps this is what I wanted to tell you most. Your story does not need to be perfect to be remarkable.

Some dreams changed. Some chapters remained unfinished. But the woman who dreamed them never stopped growing.

And I hope you know how deeply I admire that woman.

You were born on a day when an entire country celebrates freedom. And somehow, you grew into someone who taught me the value of thinking, choosing, learning, and standing by what I believe in.

If I ever become even a little bit of the person you hoped I would become, a part of that will always belong to you.

With all my love,
Your daughter

✦

Happy Birthday, Maa.

15 August
